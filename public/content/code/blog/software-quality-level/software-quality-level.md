# Software Management and Quality

## Introduction

**Definition**: "The degree to which a system, component, or process complies with its respective requirements" or "The degree to which a system, component, or process complies with the needs and expectations of customers or users."

## Attributes

- functionality,
- reliability,
- usability,
- efficiency,
- maintainability, and
- portability.

### Functionality

Does the software have the functionalities that meet what the user wants?

#### Categories

- Suitability: providing a set of functions for the user;

- Accuracy: providing the necessary precision;

- Interoperability: interacting with other systems;

- Security: protecting information and data;

- Compliance: in accordance with legally mandated standards.

### Reliability

Can it maintain good performance according to the context?

#### Categories

- Maturity: avoiding failures caused by problems in the software;

- Fault tolerance: maintaining a level of performance in cases of problems;

- Recoverability: in case of failures, returning to normal performance and recovering data;

- Compliance: laws, standards, and rules related to reliability;

### Usability

Is the software understandable, usable, and appealing to the user?

#### Categories

- Understandability: enables comprehension by the user;

- Learnability: enables the user to learn about the application;

- Operability: enables the user to learn to operate it;

- Protection against user errors: how it prevents user errors;

- Attractiveness: ability to attract a potential user;

- Accessibility: inclusive software practice;

- Compliance: laws, rules, and regulations related to usability.

### Efficiency

Are execution time and resources consistent with performance?

#### Categories

- Time behavior: response time within specifications;

- Resource utilization: measures resource usage;

- Compliance: laws, rules, and regulations related to efficiency.

### Maintainability

How modifiable the software is, taking into account new features, improvements, and fixes.

#### Categories

- Analyzability: observing and identifying problems/failures;

- Changeability: ease of modification;

- Stability: ability to avoid defects as new modifications are made;

- Testability: ability to test the system;

- Compliance: laws, rules, and regulations related to maintainability.

### Portability

Can the system be transferred to another environment?

#### Categories

- Adaptability: adapting to different environments;

- Installability: installing in a new environment;

- Coexistence: how easily it operates alongside other software in the same environment;

- Replaceability: ability to replace another system;

- Compliance: laws, rules, and regulations related to portability.

### Quality in use

Whether the software allows users to achieve their goals effectively and safely.

#### Categories

- Effectiveness: how effective the software is in allowing the user to achieve the goal;

- Productivity: how much of the resources are used to achieve the goal;

- Safety: acceptable levels of risk in relation to effectiveness;

- Compliance: laws, rules, and regulations related to quality in use.
