# Fog

### The fog is rising!

The fog is a force of nature. It is extremelly toxic. It is currently invading most small low islands.
The [[Academy]] suggests that the fog is rising is because of the constant use of the [[Wind Calling]]. It causes the wind to "pull" the fog.

[[Protectors]] are currently risking their lives to push the fog away and save villages. The [[Capital]] forces them to fight for the land (instead of evacuate people) because the [[Lowlands]] has the richest soil of them all.
