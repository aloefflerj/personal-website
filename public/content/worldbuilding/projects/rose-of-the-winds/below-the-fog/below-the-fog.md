# Below the Fog

The region [[Below the Fog]] is a region that is very dangerous. Very few venture those lands. The [[Fog]] there is highly dense and extremelly toxic.

Usually [[Engineer]] that works with maintenance have to go there to repair the base of buildings.
Also, [[Seekers]] that go there are extremelly well paid, thought it is very rare that a [[Land Lord]] or [[Land Mayor]] risk their money with that.

It is an interesting area, since you can see past lifes as you go deeper. It is a time travel experience. Forgotten knowlege are hidden on those lands.
