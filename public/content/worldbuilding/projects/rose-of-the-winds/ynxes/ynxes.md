# Ynxes

Ynxes are long forgottend animals/creatures that vibrate the air throught the larynx. The sound produced by them makes the air around to flow. They were the natural callers of the wind, on the phenomenon known as [[Wind Calling]].
Present [[Instruments]] were heavingly inspired by their anatomy, and some were actually built when [[Ynxes]] livend amongst humans.
Some other [[Instruments]] were based on fossilized larynxes of [[Ynxes]].
