# Seekers

[[Seekers]] are people that works as land searchers. Usually they are financed by some [[Land Lord]] or [[Land Mayor]].
They don't need, but are usually graduated by the [[Academy]] in the [[Navigation Field]].
They need to have a very good notion of [[Algorythm]] writing in [[Punch Cards]] to play in the [[Calliope]]. Or they need to be excelent players of [[Magical Instruments]], to deal with eventual misfortunes. Usually both of these types go on a trip.
