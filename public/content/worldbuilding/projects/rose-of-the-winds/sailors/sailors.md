# Sailors

[[Sailors]] are people that works piloting [[Balloons]]. It is a more certain way of making a live.
They don't need, but are usually graduated by the [[Academy]] in the [[Navigation Field]].
They need to have a very good notion of [[Algorythm]] writing in [[Punch Cards]] to play in the [[Calliope]]. Also, they need to be good players of [[Magical Instruments]].

Travelling usually takes two [[Sailors]], one good at [[Algorythm]] and one good at playing [[Magical Instruments]]. Travels are usually accompained of one [[Protectors]] and [[Police Force]].
