# Civil Engineering

[[Civil Engineering]] is a field of study taught in the [[Academy]]. As the name suggests, it is a field that study the makings of civil constructions. It is heavily guided by the fact that houses and constructions need to be protected from the [[Fog]].

Engineers usualy have to think how to build tall buildings to avoid the [[Fog]]. The most expensive engineers are the ones that work on tall buildings and the ones that do low level maintenance [[Below the Fog]] ([[Fog]]). Usually a specialized group goes there (a [[Protectors]] given by the [[Capital]], a [[Sailors]]).]
