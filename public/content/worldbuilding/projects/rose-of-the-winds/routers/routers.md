# Routers

[[Routers]] are people that work as route making. They need to be extremely proficient in the knowledge of [[Algorythm]], since it is used with [[Punch Cards]] in the [[Calliope]] to control the [[Balloons]]. It is well paid, especially if it is a very good router. 

They are one of the three [[Navigators]] job. They are put in the [[Navigation]] aspect because the need to navigate a lot to know the ways in which they will write the [[Algorythm]].

Only [[Academy]] graduated people are allowed.
